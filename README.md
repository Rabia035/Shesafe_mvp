# Shesafe_mvp
Female commute safety MVP designed for Her-Hackathon 2026.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

According to our primary commuter safety survey conducted among female university students, **[68.4%]** rely on local public vans for their daily commute, yet **[78%]** parents have no real-time tracking, and **[84.2%]** strongly demanded verified drivers and institutional oversight.

- **Lack of Real-Time Tracking:** Families and guardians have no reliable way to monitor the live route or exact location of the vehicle once the passenger boards.
- **Unverified Drivers & Vehicles:** Commuters are forced to travel with unverified drivers, creating anxiety and vulnerability during transit—especially during late hours or route deviations.
- **Delayed Emergency Response:** In critical situations, victims struggle to quickly share their exact coordinates and vehicle details with guardians or authorities.
- **Communication Gap:** Existing ride-hailing apps are either too expensive for daily student commutes or unavailable for local van routes, leaving a massive safety gap in everyday public transit.

**SheSafe** bridges this gap by providing a dedicated, accessible commute safety platform that connects students, guardians, and verified drivers through live route tracking, driver verification, and instant SOS alerts.
